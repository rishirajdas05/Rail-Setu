# How fares are estimated

The 2016 dataset has no distances or real fares, so RailSetu estimates them. It
approximates the distance of your segment from the journey time and the train's
typical speed, then applies a per kilometre rate for the class plus a reservation
charge, with a premium multiplier for fast trains like Rajdhani and Shatabdi.
Tatkal adds a surcharge.

Indicative per kilometre rates by class: 1A about 4.5, EC about 3.2, 2A about 2.6,
3A about 1.8, CC about 1.6, SL about 0.6, 2S about 0.45.

All fares in RailSetu are indicative estimates for demonstration and will not match
official IRCTC fares.
