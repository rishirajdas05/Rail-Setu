# How to use RailSetu

- Search trains: on the home page enter a From station and a To station (a code
  like NDLS or a city name like Delhi) and a date, then Search. Results show every
  train on that route with departure, arrival, and duration. You can sort by
  Recommended (machine learning), departure, arrival, or duration, and filter by
  type.
- Seat availability and fare: open a train from the results to see indicative
  availability and fares per class, with General and Tatkal options.
- Book: from a train, choose class and quota and book. You get a 10 digit PNR.
- PNR status: use the PNR page or the PNR Status tile to check any PNR.
- Live status: the Running Status tile shows live position and delay when the live
  provider is configured.
- Coaches and platform: the Coaches page shows coach layout, a seat or berth map,
  and the boarding platform.
- Predict: the Predict page estimates waitlist confirmation chances.
