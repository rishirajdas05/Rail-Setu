# Travel classes and their codes

- 1A: First AC, the most premium, private cabins, lower and upper berths.
- 2A: AC 2 Tier, 2 berths per bay plus side berths, curtains, fairly spacious.
- 3A: AC 3 Tier, 3 berths stacked (lower, middle, upper) plus side berths.
- SL: Sleeper, non AC, 3 berths stacked, the cheapest reserved sleeping option.
- CC: AC Chair Car, seats not berths, used on day trains like Shatabdi.
- EC: Executive Chair Car, premium seating, usually 2 plus 2.
- 2S: Second Sitting, basic non AC seats.

Which classes a train offers depends on its type. For example Rajdhani trains run
AC classes (3A, 2A, 1A), Shatabdi trains run chair classes (CC, EC), and ordinary
Express and Superfast trains usually offer SL, 3A, and 2A.
