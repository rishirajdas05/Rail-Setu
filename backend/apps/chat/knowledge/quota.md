# Quotas: General and Tatkal

- General (GN) is the normal booking quota, open well in advance.
- Tatkal (TQ) is an emergency quota for last minute travel, opened one day before
  the journey. Tatkal tickets cost more because a Tatkal surcharge is added on top
  of the base fare.

Indicative Tatkal surcharges by class in RailSetu: 2S adds about 15, SL about 150,
CC about 175, 3A about 350, 2A about 450, EC about 450. First AC (1A) does not have
a Tatkal quota. These figures are indicative, not official.

Tatkal availability is usually much tighter than General, so Tatkal tickets often
end up RAC or waitlisted.
