# Cancellation and refunds

You can cancel a booking from your account page. RailSetu follows IRCTC style rules
for the refund (indicative):

- Confirmed tickets cancelled more than 48 hours before departure: a flat
  cancellation charge per passenger by class is deducted.
- Between 48 and 12 hours before departure: about 25 percent of the fare is
  deducted.
- Between 12 and 4 hours before departure: about 50 percent is deducted.
- RAC or waitlisted tickets cancelled in time: a small clerkage charge is deducted.

Cancelling is final and the refund amount is shown when you cancel. These rules are
indicative and simplified.

## How to cancel
Go to the Account page, find the booking, and use the Cancel button. The refund
estimate appears in place after cancelling.
